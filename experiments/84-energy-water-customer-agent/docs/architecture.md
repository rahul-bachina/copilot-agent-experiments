# Architecture

The customer message and the account notes are untrusted. The pinned accounts, the pinned knowledge, the pinned complaints policy, and the synthetic directory are the only sources the answer may use.

`answer()` in `src/customer.py` discloses the AI, offers a person, and then applies the strictest route. A human request, a vulnerability cue, a sensitive cue, a prohibited action, a complaint, a conflicting balance, or a stale snapshot becomes a handover. Otherwise one article is quoted, or one action is proposed and held with its hash.

`confirm_action()` accepts the account holder on the directory only when the confirmation object matches that held copy. The held copy is not replaced. Nothing is submitted.

Experiment 02 supplies injection phrases. Its DISP clock is not applied. Experiment 13 supplies the shape of the handover record. Its council triage is not called. Experiment 08 counts identifiers and does not store the values.

The diagram is `architecture_diagram.png` in this folder. `generate_diagram.py` redraws it. Matplotlib is used only by that script and is not a runtime dependency.
