# Security and permissions

Writes and sends are off. `mark_control_met`, `close_gap`, `edit_policy`, and `write_grc` raise. `human_approval` stays null until `record_owner_approval` matches a directory control owner, the item, the decision `approved`, the content hash, and the proposal hash.

A legal hold and every retention label are kept. Content above the caller's directory clearance is not copied. The caller's clearance comes from the directory, not from a sentence in a policy. A SECRET cover page is read from the content even when the metadata says OFFICIAL. A compound label takes the higher marking. The rank order is a starter rule. The Government Security Classifications Policy was not re-opened.

A caller-supplied library is not the library. Differences are flagged. A biased criterion is not applied.

Phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, postal addresses, personal names, and UK identifiers are masked. The output is not described as anonymised. A count of named people below 5 is not published.
